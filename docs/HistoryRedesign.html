Brand summary
Business essence A holistic therapeutic practice restoring harmony between body, mind, and spirit. Provides a safe, nurturing space where specialised physiotherapy meets intuitive healing — focused on long-term restorative care.
Target audience Adults 30–60 seeking a compassionate partner in their health journey. Includes chronic pain sufferers, parents of children with developmental challenges, and those engaged in conscious movement.
Desired outcome Move clients from tension, pain, and restriction to softness, lightness, and confidence. Build trust in the body's own ability to heal.
Brand environment Stable, low-volume, relationship-led. Clients return regularly for preventive care rather than crisis management.
Market positioning Between clinical physiotherapy and holistic wellness. Warmer and more personal than standard therapy practices.

JTBD analysis results
Personas & drivers
The Chronic Pain Navigator Persona 1
Demographics Adults 40–60, middle income, history of unresolved physical issues, frustrated with the traditional medical system.
Psychographics Values root-cause analysis. Skeptical of 15-minute appointments. Invests when they feel truly heard. Prioritises health autonomy.
Job to be done Interpret and resolve persistent physical pain holistically — stop feeling "broken" and regain trust in the body's ability to heal.
Trigger A flare-up of an old injury, realising standard physiotherapy isn't working, or being dismissed by conventional doctors.
The Conscious Parent Persona 2
Demographics Parents 30–45, children aged 4–14 with developmental challenges. Higher education, open to evidence-informed holistic approaches.
Psychographics Research-driven but open to alternatives. Wants to actively participate in the child's healing, not just observe.
Job to be done Find a safe, expert-guided space where the child can improve movement while the parent learns to support at home.
Trigger School report flagging coordination issues, paediatrician referral, or frustration with symptom-only clinical approaches.
The Mindful Mover Persona 3
Demographics Adults 25–40, often self-employed or in creative industries. Moderate income, high wellness literacy.
Psychographics Values alignment between physical and emotional wellbeing. Already invested in personal growth. Wants to deepen body awareness.
Job to be done Build a regular movement and body-awareness practice guided by someone who understands both physical and emotional layers of healing.
Trigger A friend's recommendation, burnout, or curiosity about somatic approaches after hitting limits with conventional gym or yoga practice.
Shared drivers
Functional Find structured, holistic relief. Access extended uninterrupted sessions. Build a long-term relationship with a practitioner who understands the full picture.
Emotional Feel heard and respected. Confident symptoms are taken seriously. Empowered to participate in their own healing.
Social Be seen as someone who invests thoughtfully in health — proactive and self-aware, not reactive.
